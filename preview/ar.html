How it works

  1. Stand on the roof. Move the phone slowly until a ring sits on the surface.
  2. Put the ring on the real corner A and tap the screen.
  3. Walk to corner B along the long edge and tap again.
  4. The panels, roof outline (red) and keep-out zones (orange) snap into place.
