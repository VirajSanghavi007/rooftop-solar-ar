How it works

  1. Move the phone slowly until a ring sits on the roof surface.
  2. Tap corner A, then corner B: two ends of the sunny edge (south side in India). Tilted panels face this edge.
  3. Keep walking around the edge, tapping every corner in order (L-shapes and notches are fine). Finish by tapping near corner A again, or press Close roof. A plain 4-corner roof can be tapped in any order.
  4. Real-size panels (1.722 × 1.134 m, 400 W) fill the roof. A smaller roof gets fewer panels; the panels never shrink.
  5. Water tank, stair room, AC unit? Press Add obstacle, tap around it (or just two opposite corners for a box), then Finish obstacle. Panels keep the clearance away from it.

Latitude sets the winter-noon sun height, which sets the gap between tilted rows so they don't shade each other.
